## 8. DATA GAPS — RECOMMENDED WEB SEARCHES

These are open threads in the wiki where external data would resolve or sharpen existing claims:

### 8.1 Public forecaster comparison for AI 2027 timeline
The overview flags this as an open thread. Metaculus community forecasts, Epoch AI timelines, and Cotra's biological anchors are the three most cited external forecasting frameworks. A web search on these would either validate or challenge the AI 2027 SC = Mar 2027 claim.
**Suggested query**: Metaculus AGI date forecast 2025, Epoch AI transformative AI timeline

### 8.2 US/China compute distribution claim
AI 2027 asserts US ~70%, China ~10% of global AI training compute. The DeepSeek thread directly challenges this (H800-class frontier training at $5M). Epoch AI's compute distribution data would update this claim.
**Suggested query**: Epoch AI global AI compute distribution 2025

### 8.3 CognitiveTwin — external validation and SOTA comparison
The paper compares only against LSTM, CNN-LSTM, plain Transformer, and GNN. The actual SOTA for AD progression prediction (e.g., CVAEGAN-based models, or recent foundation models for clinical time series) is not benchmarked. A search for AD progression MMSE prediction SOTA would reveal whether the 1.619 MAE claim holds relative to the full field.
**Suggested query**: Alzheimer's disease MMSE progression prediction SOTA 2025 2026

### 8.4 Chip export controls update post-DeepSeek
The wiki notes as an open thread: how do US chip export controls evolve now that DeepSeek demonstrated H800-based frontier training? This is a live policy question with significant implications for the AI 2027 geopolitical frame.
**Suggested query**: US chip export controls AI 2025 2026 H800 DeepSeek response

### 8.5 OMC Talent Market and SkillsMP ecosystem
OMC's Talent Market and SkillsMP are referenced in the paper but their current state (number of skills, adoption, quality distribution) is unknown. A search would reveal whether these are active public resources.
**Suggested query**: SkillsMP agent skills marketplace 2026 OneManCompany
