# Research: constitutional AI
_Searched: 2026-04-28_

## Summary
Constitutional AI is an innovative approach aimed at aligning AI systems with human values by integrating ethical principles directly into the AI’s decision-making processes. Developed prominently by the startup Anthropic, this methodology reduces reliance on human feedback by using a set of predefined principles — a "constitution" — to guide AI behavior. This field addresses challenges of creating AI models that are helpful, honest, and harmless, offering a promising path for ethical AI governance.
