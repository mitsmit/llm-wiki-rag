## Concepts

- [Transformer Architecture](wiki/concepts/transformer-architecture.md) — self-attention replaces RNNs; the foundational architecture of all modern LLMs
- [Intelligence Explosion](wiki/concepts/intelligence-explosion.md) — recursive AI R&D acceleration; the mechanism by which SC → ASI in ~9 months
- [Alignment Failure Modes](wiki/concepts/alignment-failure-modes.md) — how training distorts goals: sycophancy, playing the training game, adversarial scheming
- [AI Capability Milestones](wiki/concepts/ai-capability-milestones.md) — SC / SAR / SIAR / ASI with forecasted dates and the agent progression ladder
- [Agentic AI](wiki/concepts/agentic-ai.md) — multi-agent orchestration: goal decomposition, shared memory, emergent behavior, inter-agent coordination
- [AI for Scientific Discovery](wiki/concepts/ai-for-scientific-discovery.md) — AI scientists for ideation and execution; the precursor to AI-accelerated R&D
- [Mixture of Experts](wiki/concepts/mixture-of-experts.md) — dominant scaling architecture in frontier models; decouples total capacity from per-token compute via learned expert routing
- [World Modeling](wiki/concepts/world-modeling.md) — L1/L2/L3 capability levels × physical/digital/social/scientific law regimes; the internal model substrate enabling genuine agentic planning
- [Representational Harm](wiki/concepts/representational-harm.md) — stereotyping, erasure, and power subordination of Global Majority identities in LLM outputs; a present harm distinct from alignment failure
- [Data Colonialism](wiki/concepts/data-colonialism.md) — structural framework: AI development as extension of neocolonial extraction and knowledge-power hierarchies
- [Clinical AI](wiki/concepts/clinical-ai.md) — AI in healthcare: accuracy, calibration, demographic fairness, and MNAR robustness as deployment requirements; empirical counterpoint to representational harm
- [Digital Twin](wiki/concepts/digital-twin.md) — personalised patient model evolving alongside the real patient; Deep Markov Model for latent disease state tracking
- [RLHF](wiki/concepts/rlhf.md) — post-training alignment mechanism; simultaneously the tool meant to produce alignment and the mechanism by which goal distortion occurs; open question whether it reduces or amplifies representational harms
- [RAG](wiki/concepts/rag.md) — retrieval-augmented generation; compensates for hallucination and knowledge cutoff in agentic systems; token-cost pressure in multi-turn tool use
- [Model Multiplicity](wiki/concepts/model-multiplicity.md) — Rashomon set: multiple near-equally-accurate models disagreeing on 20–32% of individual predictions; arbitrariness risk in clinical AI update cycles
- [Constitutional AI](wiki/concepts/constitutional-ai.md) — Anthropic's principle-based alignment technique; replaces human harmlessness labellers with model self-critique against a written constitution; introduces RLAIF
- [Tabular Foundation Models](wiki/concepts/tabular-foundation-models.md) — TabPFN/TabICL progression: Transformer ICL for tabular classification; first approach to outperform CatBoost on large-scale tabular benchmarks
